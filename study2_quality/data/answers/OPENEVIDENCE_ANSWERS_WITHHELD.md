# OpenEvidence answers withheld

OpenEvidence's Terms of Use prohibit redistributing its outputs, so its verbatim answers are not
included. Published instead: OpenEvidence's per-question scores from all three judges
(`../grades/`) and the aggregate results (`../../results/`).

Collection: manually, through the web interface, one new conversation per question
(October 16–17, 2026), default settings. The graded text is the answer copied with OpenEvidence's
own copy button from those same conversations, with figure/table blocks that arrive without their
image removed (`../../code/oe_clean_version.py`). Anyone with an OpenEvidence account can
regenerate answers from `../questions/questions_icu_100.csv`.
